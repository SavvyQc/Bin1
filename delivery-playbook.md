# Intro Package Delivery Playbook

## Objective
Get your intro package into the hands of the right person at each branch and capture a clean follow-up path.

## Success Criteria (per stop)
- [ ] Package delivered to: Branch Manager or Business Banking / Commercial RM
- [ ] Captured: name + role + email/phone (or routing path)
- [ ] Tagged branch: warm / neutral / cold + short note why

## Talk Track

### Gatekeeper Script (30 seconds)
> "Hi -- can I leave an introduction packet for your Branch Manager or business banking team?"

**If asked "what is it?":**
> "I'm a local Certified General Appraiser. This is a quick overview of what I do and the best contact path if your lenders need appraisal support."

### Manager Script (if you get them)
> "I'm doing a short round this week to introduce myself to branches in Lancaster and Ephrata. Who's the right person for appraisal vendor relationships here -- branch, business banking, or a centralized department?"

### The One Question That Matters
> "Is appraisal ordering handled here at the branch or centralized?"

If centralized, ask for:
- Department name
- Email address
- Vendor onboarding process

---

## Stop Workflow (2 minutes max)

1. Walk in, ask for manager/business banking
2. Deliver packet
3. Ask: "Is appraisal ordering handled here or centralized?"
4. **Log immediately** (don't trust memory):
   - Name/role
   - What they said
   - Follow-up instruction
   - Temperature: warm / neutral / cold

## Follow-Up System

>

>
> Best,
> [Your Name]
> [Phone] | [Email]

### Two-Touch Cadence
- **Touch 1**: Same day or next morning (email above)
- **Touch 2**: 10-14 days later ("Just checking the right contact path")

### Touch 2 Template

## Intro Package Contents

1. **One-page intro** -- who you are, what you do, geography served, why it matters to their clients
2. **Service menu** -- bullet list of appraisal types + turnaround norms + compliance credibility
3. **Proof / credibility** -- licenses, coverage area, E&O note, representative client types
4. **Business card**
5. **Optional**: "When a bank needs an appraisal" cheat sheet (SBA / CRE refi / portfolio / REO)

### Packaging
- Slim envelope or branded folder
- Label front: **"For: Branch Manager / Business Banking"**
- Your phone + email on label

---

## Risk Controls

- Verify hours for each branch before the day starts
- Avoid lunch hour (12:00-1:30) for manager meetings
- If told "centralized" -- always ask for department name + email + vendor onboarding process
- Don't linger past 2 minutes unless genuine conversation opens
- Log every stop immediately -- memory degrades fast across 10+ branches
