## Common Scenarios Where We Help

**New commercial acquisition financing**
Borrower buying office, retail, industrial, or mixed-use property. Lender needs certified general appraisal for credit file.

**SBA loan origination**
SBA SOP requires independent appraisal by state-certified appraiser. Turnaround expectations are often tight.

**Portfolio loan review / renewal**
Existing loans approaching maturity or covenant test. Updated valuation supports credit decision.

**Construction draw / progress inspection**
Lender needs periodic inspection to verify completion percentage before releasing draw funds.

**REO / foreclosure disposition**
Bank-owned property needs current market value for disposition strategy or accounting.

**Refinance (commercial or residential)**
Rate/term or cash-out refi requires updated valuation, especially if prior appraisal > 12 months old.
